Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by GetSetGo Apps ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled when you use our applications.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Our applications do not require user account registration, login, or the submission of personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

However, to provide, maintain, and monetize our services, our applications integrate third-party SDKs that automatically collect certain non-personal device information and usage analytics.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

We use Start.io (StartApp Inc.) to display advertisements within our applications. Start.io may collect and process data to deliver personalized and non-personalized ads, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

Data collected by Start.io may include:

  • Device and Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device type, operating system version, and system language.
  • Coarse Location Data: Non-precise location inferred from IP address or network data for geographical ad targeting.
  • App Interaction & Performance: Ad viewability, click interactions, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.

For more details on how Start.io handles collected data, please review the Start.io Privacy Policy.

3. Data Security and Encryption

All data transmitted between our applications, third-party ad networks, and server endpoints is encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) protocols.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for general audiences and are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application services. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
